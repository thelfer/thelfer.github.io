evaluate a material property at the points of a batch (see mfront::gpmp::evaluateBatch).

The execution policy must be a SyclBackendExecutionPolicy. With the NONE data transfer policy, the fields must be accessible by the device associated with its queue (shared, device or host USM allocations, or any memory if the device is the host). With the AUTOMATIC policy, the arguments, the output, the statuses of the points and the statistics which are not accessible are copied in device memory. The output is copied in both directions, so that the values between two values of a strided output are kept. The evaluation is asynchronous if the policy requests it and if no field is copied.

The batch, the execution policy and the queue are checked before any transfer, and checked again, at no cost, by the functions called after the transfers.
