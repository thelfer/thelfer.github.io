struct  mfront::gpmp::PointStatus
 status of the evaluation of a material property at a point, computed by the kernels. With atomic operations, it is merged in the statistics of the evaluation only if an event occurred, so that the cost of the reporting is null when the evaluation succeeds. The reductions merge the statuses of all the points. More...
