Detailed Description

template<typename real>
struct mfront::generic_parallel::BatchStatistics< real >

statistics of the computations at one point or at a group of points of a batch, combined by parallel reductions. The keys are built by makeKey.

The time step scaling factor is a statistics of the computations which may reduce the time step (see hasTimeStepScalingFactor): the material properties leave it to its neutral value.
