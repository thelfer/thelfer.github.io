Detailed Description

statistics of the evaluation of a material property on a batch of points (copy of the mfront_gpmp_Statistics structure of TFEL). The device only updates them when an event occurs at a point (the stlpar backend merges the statuses of the points by a reduction on the host). They are initialized by mgis_mp_initializeBatchStatistics and analysed by the report function of the material property.
