Detailed Description

template<typename Evaluation, size_type N>
struct mfront::gpmp::PointEvaluator< Evaluation, N >

function object evaluating a material property at the points of a batch: the arguments are read, the evaluation at one point is called, and the output and the status of the point are written.

Template Parameters
Evaluationevaluation at one point, generated by MFront, taking the values of the arguments and the out of bounds policy, and returning a PointResult
Nnumber of arguments
