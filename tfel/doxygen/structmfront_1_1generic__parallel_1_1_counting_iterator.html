Detailed Description

template<std::integral IndexType>
struct mfront::generic_parallel::CountingIterator< IndexType >

a random access iterator over a range of indices.

std::views::iota can't be used with the parallel algorithms of some implementations of the standard library: the category of its iterators, in the sense of the C++17 iterators, is not std::random_access_iterator_tag and the algorithms then run sequentially (this is the case of libstdc++).
