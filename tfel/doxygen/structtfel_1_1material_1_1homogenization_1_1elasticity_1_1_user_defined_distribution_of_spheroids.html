Detailed Description

template<tfel::math::ScalarConcept StressType>
requires (tfel::math::checkUnitCompatibility< tfel::math::unit::Stress, StressType>())
struct tfel::material::homogenization::elasticity::UserDefinedDistributionOfSpheroids< StressType >

This struct represents a distribution of spheroidal inclusions whose orientation tensors A2 and A4 are given. A2 is the average of n^n and A4 the average of n^n^n^n.
