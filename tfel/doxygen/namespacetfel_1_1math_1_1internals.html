advance an iterator by the given number of positions.

The std::advance, std::next and std::prev functions of libstdc++ can't be used in device code: they call the __builtin_constant_p built-in, which nvcc treats as a host function, and nvcc 13.0 silently compiles them as functions doing nothing.
