Detailed Description

modifier changing the basis of the tensorial values computed by an evaluator in the backwards direction, i.e. using the transpose of the given rotation matrix

This is the counterpart of RotateModifier: where the latter gives the components of the values in the basis whose vectors are the columns of the rotation matrix, the former gives their components in the initial basis knowing their components in that basis.
