Detailed Description

result of the search of the behaviour integrator providing a given quantity (gradient, thermodynamic force or internal state variable) on a given location.

Several behaviour integrators can be defined on the same location. Each of them may or may not be associated with a material. A quantity is generally defined by only one of those materials. The search is successful if, and only if, exactly one behaviour integrator associated with a material provides the requested quantity.
