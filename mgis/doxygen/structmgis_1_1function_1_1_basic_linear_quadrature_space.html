Detailed Description

template<size_type N>
requires (N > 0)
struct mgis::function::BasicLinearQuadratureSpace< N >

a minimal implementation of a linear quadrature space

The elements of such a space are the quadrature points of all the cells of the space. Given the index e of a cell and the index i of one of its quadrature points, the offset of the corresponding element is e * N + i, which is why no workspace is required to access the cells of the space.

Template Parameters
Nnumber of integration points per cell, which must be greater than zero
