evaluator computing a finite strain tangent operator from a tangent operator of another kind, given two deformation gradients and the Cauchy stress

The conversion is performed by tfel::material::convert<ResultFlag, SourceFlag, N>, applied to the values returned by the four underlying evaluators at a given integration point. Only the call operators which match the way all the underlying evaluators can be called are available.

Note
