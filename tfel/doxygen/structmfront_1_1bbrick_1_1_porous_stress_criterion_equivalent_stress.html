Detailed Description

an helper class describing the equivalent stress on which a generalised porous stress criterion is built.

A generalised porous stress criterion is a porous stress criterion in which the von Mises stress is replaced by an equivalent stress chosen by the user among the available stress criteria. This class handles the equivalent_stress option of such a criterion and generates the calls to the functions of the TFEL/Material library, which are assumed to have the following names and arguments, <name> being the name of the porous stress criterion:

  • compute<name>Stress(s, seq, f, params, seps),
  • compute<name>StressNormal(s, seq, dseq_ds, f, params, seps),
  • compute<name>StressSecondDerivative(s, seq, dseq_ds, d2seq_dsds, f, params, seps),

where seq, dseq_ds and d2seq_dsds are the equivalent stress and its first and second derivatives with respect to the stress.
