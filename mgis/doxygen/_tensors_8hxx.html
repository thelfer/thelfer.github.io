Definition Config.hxx:140

modifier computing the logarithmic strain from the deformation gradients computed by an evaluator

The values must be unsymmetric tensors, interpreted as deformation gradients F. The logarithmic strain is obtained from the Green-Lagrange strain tensor e = (F^T F - I) / 2: its eigen values vp are transformed into log(1 + 2 vp) / 2 and the result is rebuilt from the transformed eigen values and the eigen vectors of e.

Template Parameters
esolvereigen solver used to compute the eigen vectors, which defaults to tfel::math::stensor_common::TFELEIGENSOLVER
